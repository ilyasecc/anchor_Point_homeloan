Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Does using a broker cost me anything? +
No. We're paid a commission by the lender you choose, once your loan settles. There's no fee to you at any stage, and the commission doesn't change which lenders we can recommend.
How is this different from going straight to my bank? +
Your bank can only offer you their own products. We compare across our full panel of lenders, so you can see how your bank's offer actually stacks up.
What do I need to get started? +
Just a rough idea of your income, savings and what you're looking to buy or refinance. We'll tell you exactly what documents to gather once we know your situation.
Can you help if my situation is complicated? +
Self-employed income, existing debt, a patchy credit history, we see this often. It usually narrows the lender panel rather than ruling anything out.
